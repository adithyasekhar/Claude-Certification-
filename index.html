How to use it

The same approach works for all four tracks.

Sit the mock cold

Before reading anything. A score you are embarrassed by is more useful than a study plan built on a guess, because it tells you which domains you can skip.

Study by weight, not by interest

Every blueprint here shows each domain’s share of the exam as a proportional bar. Spend your time where the marks are, which is rarely where the fun is.

Build something with the weak domain

These exams lean on scenarios. Reading about termination conditions and hitting a runaway loop yourself produce very different exam performance.

Re-sit timed

Every mock is the real length under the real clock, with options shuffled each attempt. Sitting 60 questions in 120 minutes is a different skill from answering ten well.

What this is, and what it is not

It is an independent, open-source study project. Every study note, practice question, distractor and explanation was written from scratch for this repository.

It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, and it contains no real exam items. Nobody here has seen a live exam question, and none are wanted — reproducing them would breach the exam agreement you sign and would make the credential worth less for everyone holding it.

Corrections are welcome. If a note is wrong or a blueprint has moved, open an issue or a pull request. Contributing guidance is in the repository.
